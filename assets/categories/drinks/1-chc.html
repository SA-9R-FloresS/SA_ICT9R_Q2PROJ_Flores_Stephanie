Christmas Hot Cocoa


OG Recipe #1
OG Recipe #2

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity Optional
evaporated milk 1 12-ounce (340-gram) can whole milk
semisweet chocolate chips 2/3 cup (113 grams) unsweetened cocoa powder
salt a pinch granulated sugar
vanilla extract 1 tsp whipped cream (or any topping)

Instructions

# What do I do?
1 In a saucepan over medium low heat, stir the evaporated milk and chocolate.
(Using optional ingredients, stir the milk, cocoa powder, and sugar.)
2 Continue stirring until everything is well combined.
3 Stir in the remaining ingredients except the vanilla extract until all the chocolate is melted.
4 Add in the vanilla extract. Let it sit for a few minutes and then serve with toppings!
!!! Too thick? Add milk to taste. This drink can be covered and refrigerated for 3-4 days.
