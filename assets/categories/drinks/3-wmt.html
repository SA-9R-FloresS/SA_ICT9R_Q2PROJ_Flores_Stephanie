Wintermelon Milk Tea


OG Recipe

Ingredients

Wintermelon Syrup Tea Base Tapioca pearls
5 cups wintermelon, peeled and cubes (900 grams) 9 teaspoons Jasmine green tea, loose leaf 1 ¾ cups brown sugar boba pearls (300 grams)
⅔ cup brown sugar (150 grams) 6 cups water, filtered (1.5 litres) 9 cups water (2 litres)
1.8 oz. rock sugar (50 grams) 1 ½ cups whole milk
ice cubes

Instructions

# Wintermelon Syrup
1 Peel and chop fresh wintermelon into small cubes. Place them in a large bowl and sprinkle the brown sugar on top. Mix well to combine, then let it sit for 1 hour. After 1 hour, the wintermelon will produce a lot of juice.
2 Transfer the mixture (including juices) into a pot. Cook over medium heat until the liquid starts bubbling.
3 Mix in the rock sugar, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for 1 hour. Mix every 5-10 minutes to prevent the fruit from burning.
4 After 1 hour, the fruit should look translucent and the juices will be thicker, like a syrup.
5.1 Option 1: use a large sieve to remove the fruit flesh and keep only the liquid syrup.
5.2 Option 2: place the entire mixture, including fruit flesh into a food processor. Pulse until a very fine paste forms. Pass the mixture through a sieve to remove larger pieces of fruit.
6 Let the syrup cool down completely to room temperature.
# Tea Base
1 Boil filtered water in a kettle to 175°F (80°C). Place the Jasmine green tea leaves into a teapot fitted with a mesh infuser.
2 Pour the hot water over the tea leaves, cover and let steep for 3 minutes. Do not steep for longer than this as it can make the tea bitter.
3 Remove the tea leaves and discard. Cool down to room temperature, then refrigerate for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
# Tapioca Pearls + Assembly
1 Cook the brown sugar boba pearls as indicated on the package. Once cooked, transfer then to ice water. Use within a few minutes for the best chewy texture.
2 In a tall serving glass, place 2-3 tablespoons of wintermelon syrup. Adjust amount to personal preference (more syrup for a sweeter drink, less for a healthier beverage).
3 Pour cold tea over the syrup, then mix everything together to dissolve the wintermelon syrup.
4 Add in the desired amount of tapioca pearls. Recommended amount is 2-3 tablespoons of cooked boba.
!!! Top with cold milk and finish with ice cubes. Enjoy immediately!
