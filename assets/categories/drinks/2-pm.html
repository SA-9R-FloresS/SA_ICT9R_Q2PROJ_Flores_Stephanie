Peppermint Mocha


OG Recipe

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
powdered baking cocoa 3 tbsp
warm water 3 tbsp
espresso 4 oz
steamed milk 12 oz
whipped cream + red sugar crystals

Instructions

# What do I do?
1 Combine baking cocoa with warm water to create a rich syrup. Pour into a 16 oz mug.
2 Add espresso and peppermint syrup.
3 Steam milk and add to remainder of cup.
!!! Garnish with whipped cream and sugar crystals!
